# MegaRomBuilder 1.1: gameplay tweaks

Date: 2026-10-12
Status: part 1 (the level 2 charge shot) approved

## 1. Purpose

Three small changes players asked for:
- a level 2 charge shot;
- enemies that don't move;
- no delay on boss attacks.

Each part is designed and approved on its own and added to this spec as a section. The first is the level 2 charge shot, a feature a kid turns on or off for their hero. The other two are added by amendment once they are designed.

## 2. Part 1: the level 2 charge shot

### Success criteria

1. A kid can turn level 2 on or off for their hero, and it plays only when on.
2. Holding shoot longer gives a bigger, stronger shot with its own picture, and the hero shows when level 2 is ready.
3. Every game made before this change plays exactly as before.

### In the game

- **Charge shot "On"** works exactly as now:
  - holding shoot for 1 second (60 steps) charges it, and the hero blinks white;
  - letting go fires the level 1 shot (12×10, 3 damage, carrying on through the enemies it hits).
- **Charge shot "Level 2"** adds a second level:
  - Holding past 1 second reaches level 2 at 2 seconds (120 steps).
  - At level 2 the white blink is twice as fast, so the kid can tell.
  - Letting go at level 2 fires the **level 2 shot**: 16×12, 5 damage, carrying on through enemies like level 1.
  - Letting go between 1 and 2 seconds fires level 1. Letting go before 1 second fires nothing, as now.
- **The level 2 shot:**
  - It is centred on the shot line of the pose it is fired from, as level 1 is.
  - It travels at the same speed.
  - It ignores the shots-on-screen limit.
  - A release mid-slide loses the charge, as now.
- **The level checker** is unchanged: it never relies on shooting.

### The editor

- **How it plays drawer:**
  - "Charge shot" becomes one step control, **Off / On / Level 2**, styled like the other ability steps.
  - Choosing Off turns level 2 off too.
  - Each choice is one undo step.
- **A new hero picture, "Level 2 shot":**
  - It sits in the animation list after Charged shot.
  - It has a fixed size of 16×12, so it has no Size buttons (like the other shots).
  - It has frames, speed and onion skin like any animation.
  - While level 2 is off it shows "(needs level 2)", as Charged shot shows "(needs charge shot)".
- **The hero preview** demonstrates the level 2 shot when level 2 is on. It holds shoot long enough to reach level 2.

### The game file

- **Format 6:**
  - The hero's abilities gain a level 2 setting, off unless the kid turns it on.
  - The hero's look gains the Level 2 shot picture.
- **Format 5 games upgrade on open with level 2 off,** so they play exactly as before. Their Level 2 shot picture starts as a copy of their own Charged shot, centred on the 16×12 canvas, so it keeps the kid's colours.
- **New games** start with the snow pack's drawn level 2 shot.
- **A file with level 2 on but charge shot off** is refused as damaged, with a plain message.

### The assistant

- Its description of the hero says when the hero has a level 2 charge shot.
- Its hero-abilities tool can turn level 2 on and off:
  - Turning it on needs charge shot on, already or in the same change; otherwise the change is refused with a plain message.
  - Turning charge shot off turns level 2 off.

### Testing

- **The game:**
  - a release before 1 second, between 1 and 2, and after 2 seconds, with level 2 off and on;
  - the damage each shot deals;
  - the faster blink at level 2.
- **The upgrade:** a format 5 game opens with level 2 off, a Level 2 shot copied from its Charged shot, and the same play.
- **The editor:** the three-step control, Off turning level 2 off, the "(needs level 2)" note, and the new picture's fixed size.
- **The assistant:** turning level 2 on and off, refused without charge shot.
- **The browser:** a screenshot of the drawer and of the Level 2 shot picture.
