# MegaRomBuilder 1.1: gameplay tweaks

Date: 2026-10-12
Status: part 1 (the level 2 charge shot) approved and built; part 2 (the Sniper) awaiting written-spec review

## 1. Purpose

Three small changes players asked for:
- a level 2 charge shot;
- enemies that don't move (designed as the Sniper);
- no delay on boss attacks.

Each part is designed and approved on its own and added to this spec as a section. The first is the level 2 charge shot, a feature a kid turns on or off for their hero. The other two are added by amendment once they are designed.

## 2. Part 1: the level 2 charge shot

### Success criteria

1. A kid can turn level 2 on or off for their hero, and it plays only when on.
2. Holding shoot longer gives a bigger, stronger shot with its own picture, and the hero shows when level 2 is ready.
3. Every game made before this change plays exactly as before.

### In the game

- **Charge shot "On"** works exactly as now:
  - holding shoot for 1 second (60 steps) charges it, and the hero blinks white;
  - letting go fires the level 1 shot (12×10, 3 damage, carrying on through the enemies it hits).
- **Charge shot "Level 2"** adds a second level:
  - Holding past 1 second reaches level 2 at 2 seconds (120 steps).
  - At level 2 the white blink is twice as fast, so the kid can tell.
  - Letting go at level 2 fires the **level 2 shot**: 16×12, 5 damage, carrying on through enemies like level 1.
  - Letting go between 1 and 2 seconds fires level 1. Letting go before 1 second fires nothing, as now.
- **The level 2 shot:**
  - It is centred on the shot line of the pose it is fired from, as level 1 is.
  - It travels at the same speed.
  - It ignores the shots-on-screen limit.
  - A release mid-slide loses the charge, as now.
- **The level checker** is unchanged: it never relies on shooting.

### The editor

- **How it plays drawer:**
  - "Charge shot" becomes one step control, **Off / On / Level 2**, styled like the other ability steps.
  - Choosing Off turns level 2 off too.
  - Each choice is one undo step.
- **A new hero picture, "Level 2 shot":**
  - It sits in the animation list after Charged shot.
  - It has a fixed size of 16×12, so it has no Size buttons (like the other shots).
  - It has frames, speed and onion skin like any animation.
  - While level 2 is off it shows "(needs level 2)", as Charged shot shows "(needs charge shot)".
- **The hero preview** demonstrates the level 2 shot when level 2 is on. It holds shoot long enough to reach level 2.

### The game file

- **Format 6:**
  - The hero's abilities gain a level 2 setting, off unless the kid turns it on.
  - The hero's look gains the Level 2 shot picture.
- **Format 5 games upgrade on open with level 2 off,** so they play exactly as before. Their Level 2 shot picture starts as a copy of their own Charged shot, centred on the 16×12 canvas, so it keeps the kid's colours.
- **New games** start with the snow pack's drawn level 2 shot.
- **A file with level 2 on but charge shot off** is refused as damaged, with a plain message.

### The assistant

- Its description of the hero says when the hero has a level 2 charge shot.
- Its hero-abilities tool can turn level 2 on and off:
  - Turning it on needs charge shot on, already or in the same change; otherwise the change is refused with a plain message.
  - Turning charge shot off turns level 2 off.

### Testing

- **The game:**
  - a release before 1 second, between 1 and 2, and after 2 seconds, with level 2 off and on;
  - the damage each shot deals;
  - the faster blink at level 2.
- **The upgrade:** a format 5 game opens with level 2 off, a Level 2 shot copied from its Charged shot, and the same play.
- **The editor:** the three-step control, Off turning level 2 off, the "(needs level 2)" note, and the new picture's fixed size.
- **The assistant:** turning level 2 on and off, refused without charge shot.
- **The browser:** a screenshot of the drawer and of the Level 2 shot picture.

## 3. Part 2: the Sniper, an enemy that stands its ground

Playtesters asked for "enemies that don't move"; what they meant was a Sniper Joe: an enemy that
never walks, hides behind a shield that blocks everything, and lowers it to fire three quick shots
the hero has to jump over. It is a new, seventh kind of enemy, "Sniper". The Shield Guard is
unchanged.

### Success criteria

1. A kid can make their own Sniper, or place the snow pack's, and it plays as described below.
2. The only ways to hurt it are while it shoots, or from behind.
3. Every game made before this change opens and plays exactly as before.

### In the game

- **Standing:** it never walks. It stands on the ground where it is placed (falling if placed in
  the air) and always turns to face the hero.
- **Guarding:** most of the time it hides behind its shield. Shots that hit it from the front
  bounce off, every one, doing no damage; shots from behind still hurt it.
- **Shooting:**
  - Every 2 seconds (always: it has no Shoots setting) it lowers the shield.
  - It fires three shots straight ahead, 12 steps (a fifth of a second) apart.
  - It stays open for 1 second (60 steps) in all, then raises the shield.
  - While open, shots hit it from any side.
- **The shots** fly level from its shot point (so the kid decides their height), at the usual enemy
  shot speed, doing its Damage. The snow Sniper fires low, so the hero jumps over them.
- **Toughness:** every enemy's Health choices become 1, 2, 3, 5, 8 and 12. A new Sniper starts at 8.
- **The level checker** treats it as an enemy like the others, and counts it as harder than a
  Shield Guard in its difficulty score.

### The editor

- "Sniper" is the seventh choice in New enemy's "Choose how it behaves", and when changing an
  enemy's behaviour. Changing to or from a Sniper keeps the drawing, like other behaviour changes.
- Its How it plays settings are Health and Damage (no Speed, no Shoots).
- Its poses are **Guard** (shield up) and **Shoot** (shield lowered), each with its own picture
  size, frames and speed like every enemy. The Shot point tool is offered on Shoot only, the pose it
  fires from.
- The Health control gains 8 and 12, for every enemy.

### The snow pack

- A drawn snow Sniper (a shield-up picture and a shooting picture) is the starting look for a kid's
  own Sniper.
- It is also a ready-made enemy, "Sniper", in the level editor's Enemies and items palette, placed
  like the Shield Guard.

### The assistant

- It knows the Sniper: "a sniper that hides behind its shield, then fires three low shots you jump
  over". It can place the snow Sniper, make a kid's own Sniper, and use the new health steps.

### The game file

- No new format: older games have no Snipers, and nothing in them changes.

### Testing

- **The game:** shots bounce off the shield from the front; it is hurt from behind, and from any
  side while shooting; a burst is three level shots 12 steps apart, every 2 seconds; it turns to
  face the hero and never walks.
- **The model:** the new behaviour's poses, settings, firing pose and health steps.
- **The editor:** making a Sniper, its two poses, the Shot point only on Shoot, the Health steps.
- **The assistant:** placing and describing the Sniper.
- **The browser:** a screenshot of a Sniper in the editor and in a level.
